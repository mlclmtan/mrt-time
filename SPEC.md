# MRT Time — v2.2 spec

Public web app (PWA) that predicts Singapore MRT train arrivals from timed platform-board readings (no operator live-arrival feed exists), plans door-to-door commutes with transfers, and doubles as a **real marketing/analytics practice product** (GA4, GTM, ads tracking, KOL tracking, SEO, experiments).

Live at `mrt.91208274.xyz`. Repo is PUBLIC: never commit personal data (home/office addresses, emails, the owner's real observations/trip logs). Personal data lives only in the DB, seeded from a gitignored file.

## 1. Stack
- Next.js 16 (App Router) on Vercel Hobby, TypeScript, Tailwind 4, Drizzle + Neon Postgres (`@neondatabase/serverless`), Better Auth (Google sign-in primary, passkey optional), zod, vitest. pnpm.
- Sibling reference (read-only): `~/code/bus` (same stack, auth allowlist, PWA, version banner, push, QStash, multi-leg trips with train legs).
- All third-party IDs/keys via env; every integration is a no-op when its env is unset. `pnpm build` passes with no env.

## 2. Users & access
- **Public (no login):** station boards, journey planner, landing/SEO pages, accuracy page, **Log a train** — what the platform screen shows (inline on station pages or `/add`; crowdsourced, rate-limited; internally a "reading").
- **Signed-in owner (`ALLOWED_EMAILS`):** personal commute (saved places + journeys), one-tap logging, admin dashboard, moderation of public readings, campaign/KOL/experiment management.
- **Analysts (`ANALYST_EMAILS`, optional):** read-only access to `/admin` analytics (for a marketing collaborator). Unset by default.

## 3. Network data (static, in repo)
- **Every MRT line and station** (v2.0, see §18): NSL, EWL (incl. the Changi Airport branch CG), NEL, CCL (incl. Stage 6), DTL, TEL. Data in `src/lib/network/` (`data.ts` lines/directions/details, `stations-list.ts` codes/names/coordinates); sources and licences in `docs/data-sources.md`.
- Line colours (official), service hours (approx 05:30–00:30). A direction is keyed by the destination the platform board shows and lists the **path(s)** its trains run (platform codes in running order ending at the terminal); branches are separate directions (EWL `TO_CHANGI_AIRPORT` / `TO_TANAH_MERAH`). Keys match `^TO_[A-Z][A-Z_]+$` (e.g. `TO_EAST`, `TO_MARINA_SOUTH_PIER`).
- Every direction's stops are an **explicit ordered list** in `data.ts` (never generated from code numbers: gaps NS6, NE2, CC18, TE10, TE21; CG1/CG2 leave the EWL at EW4; CC33/CC34 were CE2/CE1). The slugs, platform codes and direction keys used by stored rows and seeded journeys are frozen by a snapshot test (`__fixtures__/frozen-keys.json`): add, never rename.
- Ride minutes per hop: observed values (EW17→EW16 3, EW15→EW17 5, TE17→TE19 3, TE17→TE2 Woodlands 37; a longer observed ride is spread evenly over its stops without a figure of their own), else 2 min a stop incl. dwell. Since v2.1 **learned ride minutes** (median of ≥ 3 of the owner's own board → got-off pairs, same line and direction) win over both for propagation (not for the planner's fixed leg minutes).
- Interchange transfer minutes (platform to platform): Outram Park EW16↔TE17 **2** (observed 0–2; a 0-minute change is real), NE3↔EW16 3, NE3↔TE17 4. Every other interchange is an estimate: 3, or 4 where the walk is known to be long. Branch change at Tanah Merah: 3 (estimate). Never above 4 unless a transfer is known to be long.

## 4. Observations & prediction engine (`src/lib/predict/`, pure, tested)
- **Observation** = board reading: station, line, direction, destination, capturedAt (SGT), minutes shown for next / next-next / optional 3rd train, source (owner | public | hermes), status (approved | pending | rejected).
- Derived: actual arrival clock times; headway = gap between shown trains.
- **Trip events** = owner logs: board / alight / at_platform / tap_in / tap_out / door / desk / etc., timestamp = the moment of the tap (never a server-delayed time).
- Prediction for (station, direction, now):
  1. **Same-day anchor:** an approved reading today at that platform within 90 min → project by its headway. Since v2.1 with the error model of §15: exact only within 2 gaps of a known train (high confidence when the next train is exact), then "≈ ±N".
  2. Else **time-band headway model:** LTA-published fallback peak (weekday 07:00–09:00, 17:00–19:00) 2–3 min, off-peak 5–7 min, overridden by the median observed headway for that line/direction/time band once ≥2 readings exist (observed so far: TEL 6 min, EWL 4–6 min off-peak).
  3. **Historical clock-time repeat** (anchor from another day at a similar time-of-day/day-type, as in v0 `lib/predict.js`) is used ONLY outside unstable windows, and since v2.0 only while the learned model (§16) has too few days to decide. **Weekday 09:15–10:30 is marked unstable** (owner observed clock times don't repeat day to day): show "every ~N min" + wait range, never exact clock times from other days. The unstable-window list is data, editable in admin.
  - Output: next arrivals (clock + countdown) or a range, headway, confidence (high/medium/low) + plain reason ("from a reading 12 min ago" / "typical gap at this hour"). The planner and owner Home use this output as is.
- **Station board view-model** (`src/lib/predict/board-view.ts`, pure): station pages **always show clock times, never an interval only**. Each direction card shows the next 3 arrivals as SGT `HH:MM` with a live countdown ("10:24 · in 2 min"):
  - **Exact** (a train known today within 2 gaps and ≤ 1 min error, incl. the visitor's own just-saved reading): solid times, chip "From the platform screen", reason "From the platform screen N min ago" (v2.2 wording; internally a board reading). **Each arrival has its own ±** (v2.1): a reading's next train is exact (±1), its 2nd / 3rd trains read "≈10:29 ±2" (±1.5); trains further from what was seen widen by 0.5 min a gap.
  - **Estimate** (another day's reading at a similar time, or the typical gap at this hour): "≈10:24 ±N min" with an "Estimate" chip and the plain reason ("Typical gap ~5 min; no platform screen logged today yet"; another day's reading names the day: "Based on the platform screen at 10:21 on Mon 2 Nov; none logged today yet"). When trains were seen here today but the last one is too old to place trains (v2.1.1), the reason says so instead: "Last train seen today at 10:14 is too long ago to use; estimated from the platform screen at 10:21 on Mon 2 Nov" (or "… from the typical gap (~6 min)"). Gap-only estimates sit on a steady grid from the start of service so they count down between renders. ± = 1 min (historical; 2 if low confidence), ⌈gap/2⌉ (typical gap), and in unstable windows max(3, ⌈gap/2⌉+1) — times are still shown, with the wider band.
  - **Every ≈ time shows its ± with the unit** ("≈10:29 ±2 min"), Next and Then alike (v2.1.1); "Trains seen today" too ("(±1 min)").
  - **Confidence of another day's reading** (v2.1.1): never High; **Medium** only at the same time of day (≤ 30 min, same day type) or when the half hour is marked repeating; at a different time of day it is **Low** (`historicalAnchorConfidence`, v2 and the v1 kill switch alike).
- **Accuracy tracking:** when the owner logs `board` at a platform, store the prediction for that moment (`prediction_check`), **snapshotted before the boarding is written** (v2.1). Boardings logged more than 2 min after they happened (time moved back, Hermes) and edited ones are not scored. Since v2.0 the public `/accuracy` page uses train sightings as ground truth (§16).

## 5. Journeys (planner)
- Journey = ordered legs: walk (fixed min), ride (line, from, to, direction), transfer (fixed min at an interchange). Fixed leg minutes per saved journey are editable.
- Planner computes for a departure time (or "now"): per ride leg the catchable train (arrival at platform + prediction), the transfer catch ("tight"/"likely"/"wait ~N min"), final arrival time, plus best/worst case. "Leave by" mode for a target arrival time. "List" mode: next 6 departures with arrival times (e.g. "leave desk at 12:30" → list).
- Public: pick any two supported stations (+ optional walk minutes) → route via the network with at most 2 transfers.
- Owner (private, from DB seed): saved places Home/Office and journeys Home→Office (walk → EW17 TO_EAST → EW16 transfer → TE17 TO_BAYSHORE → TE19 → walk), Office→Home (reverse), and alternative Office→Home via Tanjong Pagar (walk → EW15 TO_WEST → EW17 → walk). Show best-door tips per leg (data from seed).
- Planner UI: time defaults to now (SGT, server-rendered so SSR and client agree), plans automatically on load and re-plans (debounced) on any input change; only visitor-initiated queries emit `planner_query`. For the owner, From/To (and walk minutes) default to the current journey's stations.
- Rain-aware hint: data.gov.sg 2-hour weather forecast (free, no key) for the relevant area → suggest the more sheltered journey variant when rain is forecast.

## 6. Owner Home & logging
- **Train times first.** Above the logging buttons:
  - Journey auto-selected by SGT time of day (before 14:00 → Home→Office, from 14:00 → Office→Home); a manual pick is remembered for 2 h (cookie `mrt_journey`).
  - **Trip card** (v2.1.1 header): "**Leave in m:ss**" while leave-by is more than 30 s away, "**Leave now**" only in the last 30 s, "**You've missed this one, next: HH:MM**" once it has passed; "Leave by HH:MM" beside it. Body: arrive ~HH:MM with best–worst, per ride leg the board clock (or range), confidence + reason ("From the platform screen 12 min ago" / "typical gap at this hour · every ~6 min"), and the best-door tip from journey data (v2.2: door icon on the first line, the whole tip wraps — never cut short).
  - **Next departures** (list mode, 6 rows; v2.2: **hidden while on board / changing trains** — it listed the train already boarded — by the phone's own taps, so also before the server catches up or while offline; back once the trip is finished or dismissed): leave at → board leg 1 (line chip + clock) → transfer catch (tight / likely / wait ~N) → board leg 2 → arrive, with a live countdown to the first leave-by. In unstable / headway-only windows rows show ranges, never exact clocks.
  - Recomputed (`GET /api/commute`, owner only) on journey change, immediately after each logging tap, right after a board reading is saved (same-day anchor), every minute, and when the first leave-by passes.
  - **Plan the rest from the tap times, never from now** (v2.1): on board → the ride ends at boarded + ride minutes; got off / tapped out → from that tap; on the platform → from now (still waiting). `startAt` may be in the past. Once now is past the **expected arrival + 15 min** (tap times + the remaining walks / changes / rides) the trip is **finished**, however old the taps are: "Arrived ~10:25?" with **Log At my desk** / **Dismiss** — asked **once, on the big next-step button** (v2.2; no second card) — and the trip card shows the whole journey planned again from now (never "On board … from ~11:19" an hour later). A platform tap with nothing after it for 90 min is an abandoned trip.
  - **One resolver** (v2.1.1, `resolveTrip` in `journey/commute.ts`) turns today's taps into "where am I / what's next" for the next-step button, the trip card's request, the server-rendered first view and `/add`'s default platform — they can never disagree. Only today's (SGT) taps count; a trip ends at the end-place tap (desk / home door / arrived); a start-place tap, or any tap after the trip was finished / abandoned, begins a new trip. **Dismiss** is remembered for the day (cookie `mrt_trip_dismissed` = the trip's last tap). With no trip under way (dismissed, ended, new day, other journey) the next step is the journey's first: **Home door** / **Left desk**.
- **One big next-step button** first on owner Home (v2.1), journey-aware ("Next: Home door" → "Next: At platform · Tiong Bahru" → "Next: I boarded · Tiong Bahru" …); once the trip is finished it reads "**Arrived ~10:25? · Log At my desk**" with **Dismiss** beside it (v2.1.1); it is sticky above the consent sheet (`--consent-h`) and the safe area, so it is never covered. Below it: the platform, the time chips and **Other steps** — I boarded / Got off, Leaving (home door / desk), At platform, Tap out, Arrived. A tap saves at once at Now (or the chip picked); the toast has Undo and "Wrong time? 10:13 · 10:12 · 10:09" (a timeline edit) for 10 s. GPS never overrides the journey step: "You're at …?" is offered on Home only when there is no journey step.
- **Offline taps** (v2.1): with no connection a tap is kept on the phone (`localStorage mrt_tap_queue`) with its own time and sent when the page opens or the device is back online (4xx dropped, 5xx retried, 12 h max). Same for public "I boarded" / "Got off".
- Board reading entry on Home: the same one-tap entry as station pages (below), preset to the next journey platform/direction, with "At a different station?" → `/add`. Optional photo is NOT stored (photos live in Notion).
- `POST /api/ingest` with bearer `INGEST_TOKEN` so Hermes can post readings/events it extracts from photos sent in chat.
### 6a. Simplified reading flow (commuter's words: which way, in how many minutes, when I saw it)
- **One-tap entry**, inline under every direction on station pages, on owner Home and in `/add`: "Board says: next train to Pasir Ris in [ ] min, then [ ] min" (+ optional third train). Large number chips 0–15 fill the next empty box (tap a filled box to clear it) plus a free number input. Time seen = now automatically; "seen at HH:MM · change" opens a time picker (default now, at most 30 min back). Save → 10 s undo toast (`DELETE /api/readings?id=`, own reading only, ≤ 2 min old).
- **No jargon** anywhere in this flow: no "headway", "source", "status", "observation", "direction key" or "dest code"; destinations are named as on the platform board (`Direction.board`, e.g. "Pasir Ris", "Joo Koon / Tuas Link", TEL "Woodlands North" / "Bayshore"). Early-ending trains (`alsoBoard`, e.g. "Springleaf") are grouped under their direction automatically.
- **`/add`** (one screen, one-handed at 393×852): 1 station (default: last used on this device, else the owner's next journey platform, else a searchable list with EN/中文 names and codes) → 2 big direction buttons named like the board (platform chip when the station has several lines) → 3 the same minute chips. `/submit` permanently redirects to `/add`.
- The form posts exactly `{ station, direction, minutes[], seenAt }` to `POST /api/readings` (device id, consented visitors only, in the `x-device-id` header). Minutes are sorted ascending.
- After saving, the card switches to exact times immediately: the reading is kept on the device for 90 min (`mrt_my_readings`) and overrides that direction's view until the server has an anchor at least as fresh; the board is re-fetched at once so other visitors get the anchor when it is approved.
- Public submissions (no login): rate limits 10/hour per device + daily IP hash and 20/hour per IP hash alone (v2.1), `seenAt` at most 30 min back, status `pending` unless consistent (±2 min) with an approved reading/prediction in the same 30-min window → auto-approve; else owner moderates in admin. The owner's readings auto-approve.

## 13. "I boarded" / "Got off" with an editable time (owner + public)
- Big **I boarded** / **Got off** buttons on owner Home, under every direction on station pages, and on `/add` (after picking the direction).
- Time chips shown as **clock times: 10:14 · 10:13 · 10:12 · 10:09 · Other time** (= Now, −1, −2, −5 min; SGT time picker; a picked time later than now means yesterday). At most **12 h back for the owner, 30 min for everyone else**. Default Now; resets to Now after each tap. One tap saves; correct it from the toast ("Wrong time?") or the timeline. Each tap tells the server `timeSource: now | chosen` (stored error ±0.5 / ±1).
- Station + direction: preselected from the current journey step (owner Home) or the station card; "You're at Tiong Bahru?" GPS chip (§17); one tap to change (platform picker: search EN/中文/code → direction buttons named like the board).
- Owner: stored as a trip event (`board` / `alight`, existing `trip_event` table) **and** a train sighting linked to it. Public: `POST /api/sightings` `{ station, direction, kind: boarded|alighted, at, timeSource? }` (at most 30 min back) → sighting with the board-reading rules: rate limits **10/hour per device + daily IP hash and 20/hour per IP hash alone** (shared with board readings; device ids are client-supplied, so they can't be the only key), approved when within ±2 min of a train the owner / Hermes put at that platform (here or propagated, recorded within 30 min) or when a **second network** (different daily IP hash) reports the same train within 1 min (both approved), else pending → **Admin → Submissions** ("Boardings to review"). - Undo toast 10 s (`DELETE /api/sightings?id=` own device/network ≤ 2 min, or `?clientId=` for the owner; owner Home uses `DELETE /api/trip-events?clientId=`). After saving, the station board re-fetches and the owner's departures recompute at once.

## 15. Train sightings + propagation along the line
- **Train sighting** = `{ platform, line, direction, at, kind: board_reading | boarded | alighted, source: owner | public | hermes, status }`. Boardings/getting off are stored in the additive table `train_sighting` (migration `0002_train_sightings`); board readings imply sightings (`capturedAt + minutes`) derived on the fly from `observation` (existing readings keep working unchanged).
- `/api/ingest` `board` / `alight` trip events create sightings (`source = hermes`). `scripts/backfill-sightings.ts` (`pnpm sightings:backfill`, idempotent) creates them for trip events logged before v2.0.
- **Error model (v2.1): nothing is exact.** Every sighting stores an error in minutes (`train_sighting.error_min`, migration `0003_review_followup`): a tap at Now ±0.5; a time moved with the chips / picker, a timeline edit, Hermes (logged later) or a board's next train ±1; a board's 2nd / 3rd train ±1.5. Rows without one (before 0003) default by kind and logging lag; `pnpm sightings:backfill` fills them.
- **Propagation** (at read time only — propagated trains are never stored): a sighting at station X (direction D, time t) is the same train at the other stops of D's path at t ± the cumulative ride minutes (§3), with error + **0.3 min a stop (0.1 on measured rides)**. It stops when the error passes **1.5 min**, at the **first possible short-turn terminal** (EW29 Joo Koon, TE4 Springleaf) unless the board named the line's real terminal, and at the terminal; a board reading naming an early terminal (Springleaf) stops there. **Boardings at a terminal are skipped** (trains wait there). Where a direction's paths split or merge (Circle Line spur and loop at Promenade) the train is only placed where every path agrees; on a loop it is placed the nearer way round (v2.2). A "Got off" takes the direction of the boarding before it (same line, ≤ 3 h). Lines with an active LTA disruption alert don't propagate. **Public** sightings and readings count only at their own platform until two networks agree (then they propagate, even while awaiting review).
- **Merge:** two candidates are the same train when they come from the same sighting or the same ride (a boarding and its getting off are linked), or when closer than max(1.5, sum of errors) min **and** under half the shortest gap (2 min peak, 5 otherwise). Keep the lowest-error one (owner / Hermes always over public, then fewer stops); never average.
- Predictions use propagated sightings as same-day anchors: the 10:14 Tiong Bahru `TO_EAST` boarding gives Outram Park 10:17, Tanjong Pagar 10:19. Reasons: "A rider got on or off a train here N min ago" / "From a train seen at Tiong Bahru N min ago"; a sighting a few stops away shows as "≈HH:MM ±N".
- **Freshness (v2.1):** a same-day time is exact only within **2 gaps** of a known train and ≤ 1 min error (stepping by the gap adds 0.5 min once, then 0.5 per further gap), then "≈ ±N"; once the next train's ± passes half the gap the same-day evidence is dropped and the next rules apply (usually a range). One tap no longer gives confident clock times for 90 min.

## 16. Predictor v2 + "Trains seen today"
- Order: (1) same-day trains known at the platform (readings here, sightings here or propagated; recorded ≤ 90 min ago, train ≤ 60 min ahead; gaps filled by the usual gap; freshness rule of §15) → (2) learned band → (3) v1 rules while there are too few scored days (v0 clock repeat outside unstable windows, else a range by the usual gap).
- **Kill switch (v2.1):** Admin → Settings → Predictor **v2 / v1** (`app_setting predictor_engine`): v1 runs the v1.2 predictor verbatim (`predict/v1.ts`: board readings at that platform only) for boards, planner, Home and `/accuracy` within a minute. With no sightings and no same-day reading, v2 predicts exactly what v1 did (tested).
- **Learning** per platform/direction, day type (weekday / Saturday / Sunday & public holiday — `PUBLIC_HOLIDAYS`: the official MOM lists for 2025–2027; a date counts as a holiday **only when listed**, unlisted dates use their weekday) and 30-min band, from all known trains (error ≤ 1 min) on earlier days: the usual gap (median; gaps spanning several trains folded back) and whether clock times **repeat**, by scoring real predictions (v2.1): each day is predicted from the previous matching day's clock times on a grid of the gap; a day is a hit when every train came within ±1 min. **Repeats** only with ≥ 4 hits in the last 5 scored days **and** a gap ≥ 4 min (project the most recent day's clock times, "Trains came at the same times on N recent days"); else **drifts** (range). **Until a band has 5 scored days the v1 rules apply, incl. the weekday 09:15–10:30 window.** (v2.0's "phase spread ≤ 1 min across ≥ 3 days" marked random phases as repeating ~48 % of the time at 2.5-min gaps.) **Admin-edited windows still force ranges.**
- Station page: **Trains seen today** per direction (newest first, ≤ 12). **Signed-in owner:** time + how we know (someone got on/off here · shown on this platform screen · seen at another station, with ± when estimated). **Everyone else (v2.2 owner privacy):** times only with a neutral "seen" — never "boarded" / "got off" / another station / source icons, for owner, Hermes and public sightings alike. Redacted on the server (`predict/privacy.ts`: `/api/board`, `/api/predict`, the public planner, the server-rendered station page), so the detail never reaches a visitor's browser; prediction reasons "someone got on or off here" / "seen at <station>" read "From a train seen N min ago" for visitors.
- `/accuracy` uses sightings as ground truth: for every approved boarding logged as it happened (not back-dated > 2 min, not edited), replay the prediction from 2 min before using only what had been **recorded** by then (never the boarding itself), score the nearest predicted train (±1 min = hit); by line, time band and station, with usual minutes off and how many were only a range.
